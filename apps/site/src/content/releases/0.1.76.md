## OAuth account setup + progressive sync

### Fixes

- **Adding an OAuth account works again.** Setting up a provider that signs in
  through your browser (such as Google) was failing right after you approved
  access, with an "unauthorized" error. The sign-in redirect is now accepted
  correctly, so OAuth account setup completes as before.

### Improvements

- **Mail shows up as it arrives during a sync.** A first or full sync no longer
  waits for the entire mailbox to download before anything appears — messages
  now stream into the list as they're fetched, and the account's progress
  reflects that. Deletions are still reconciled once at the end, so nothing is
  shown or removed incorrectly.
