## Account, compose, and outbox fixes

### Fixes

- **Accounts no longer get stuck "syncing."** Account status now comes straight
  from the engine as a live snapshot rather than being pieced together from
  update events, so a missed update can't strand an account in the syncing
  state — it always reflects the real status.
- **Settings no longer pops open on its own during a sync.** A background sync
  could briefly make the account list look empty and yank you into account
  setup; the underlying empty-flicker is fixed and the auto-open is gone.
- **The message list fills the whole window again.** It was capping at about 15
  rows no matter the window size; the list now measures its height correctly and
  shows as many messages as fit (and scrolls to the rest).
- **Compose always has a sender.** For an account whose provider doesn't return a
  sender identity, compose now falls back to your configured address, so the
  From field is filled in by default.

### Improvements

- **Outbox items you can act on.** Failed or queued items in the Outbox now have
  **Retry** and **Discard** actions, so a stuck change no longer sits there
  forever — you can re-send it or clear it out.
