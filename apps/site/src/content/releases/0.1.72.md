## Real undo/redo, and a simpler core

### Improvements

- **Undo and redo now work like a desktop app.** Press ⌘Z to step back through
  your recent actions — flag, mark read, tag, archive, trash, move — one at a
  time, and ⇧⌘Z to step forward again. The history is kept by the engine, so it
  stays correct across the whole session, and a move's "Undo" toast reverses
  exactly that action. (Permanent delete is not reversible.)
- **The open message reflects changes instantly.** Flagging or marking the
  message you're reading updates its header right away, fed live from the engine
  rather than waiting on a separate refresh.

### Under the hood

- The app no longer keeps a second, parallel copy of "what you just did" in the
  UI. Optimistic changes and their undo now come from one place — the runtime —
  removing a class of drift and flicker bugs where the displayed state and the
  synced mailbox could disagree.
