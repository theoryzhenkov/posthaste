## Faster mail, especially with attachments

### Improvements

- **Archiving, deleting, flagging, and moving no longer lag on emails with
  attachments.** These actions could take half a second or more on a message
  that carried attachments — the whole message body was being shuffled around
  just to change one flag — and are now as quick as on any other message.
- **Opening a message is quicker.** The header and details appear right away and
  the body loads on its own, rather than the whole view waiting on it — most
  noticeable on long emails and ones with inline images.
